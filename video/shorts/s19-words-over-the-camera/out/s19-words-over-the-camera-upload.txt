TITLE
Words Over the Camera: Send. Clear. Obs. Switcher. #Shorts

DESCRIPTION
Put your lyrics over a live camera, in OBS or a switcher. Add a screen, and send it over the network instead of to a monitor. Make it transparent, and turn the background off. In OBS, pick the stream, and the words sit over the camera. For a switcher, fill and key go out of one Blackmagic device.
