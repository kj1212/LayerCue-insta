TITLE
Two Screens, One Stage: Add. Words. On air. Unplug. #Shorts

DESCRIPTION
One stage, two screens: the video for the audience, the words for the singers. Open Stage output, and add a screen for the stage monitor. Turn the background off there, and the singers get the words on black. The on-air chip lists every screen, each with its own power switch. Unplug a monitor, and it picks up again as soon as it's back.

Free while it's in beta, on Mac and Windows: https://layercue.com

DESCRIPTION
