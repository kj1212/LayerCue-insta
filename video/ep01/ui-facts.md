# Episode 1 — what is actually on screen (verified on the recording sandbox, 2026-10-30)

Use these facts over the plan where they differ. Screenshots: `video/ep01/shots/*_s.png` (1600 px wide; the
`.png` without suffix is the full 3840×2100 window capture).

## Recording setup
- macOS, LayerCue in **English**. The LayerCue window fills a 1920×1080 (HiDPI) display: captured at 3840×2100 px
  (the menu bar is cropped away). Final video is 3840×2160, 30 fps.
- **Screen 1 = Virtual screen 1920×1080**, power switch ON. So the status bar says **On air 1/1 · Screen 1** the whole
  episode, and the stage shows in the tile's preview — no projector in the room. Nothing needs to be "turned on".
- No **Update** chip and no wrench in the status bar during this recording. Do not mention a version number.
- Sound device chosen: **LG HDR 4K** (shown in the status bar speaker box).
- The real pointer is visible in the recording. Clicks, hovers (tooltips after ~1 s), drags are all possible.
  Edit/Show are clicked. Sending slides can be a real click on a card or scripted.
- Editing (zoom-ins, highlight boxes, dimming, callout labels, lower-third captions) is done in After Effects.

## Window — title bar
- Traffic lights, then two small buttons: **sidebar** toggle, **Slide settings**. Wordmark **LAYERCUE** centred.

- Heading **Cue Sheets** with a **+** at its right.
- Cue sheet **Tour** (6 items): Start here 4 · Working with slides 5 · Bring it in 5 · Make it with AI 4 ·
  Stage output 5 · Libraries & cue sheets 4. (SIX guides, not five.)
- Cue sheet **Summit Day 1** (3 items): Welcome loop 1 · Welcome words 3 · Summit logo 1.

  drawer) and a **+**. Libraries: **READ ME** 6 (the same six guides), **My Stuff** 1 (New Presentation),

- The picked row is lighter with a thin indigo bar at its left. A short orange bar at the left marks the
  presentation that has something on stage (shot 02: Welcome loop, Welcome words, Summit logo all have it).

## Middle — slides (shot 03 cue sheet, 01 single presentation, 08 Summit Day 1)
- Click a cue sheet → every presentation of it, one after another, each under its own title bar (name + slide
  count). Click a presentation in a library → that one presentation only.
- Title bar buttons at the right, left→right: **+** (Add File) · chain (Link) · pencil (Edit) · stacked sheets
  (Layer for this list) · two overlapping circles (Presentation settings).
- A presentation whose Layer for this list is set shows a chip beside its name: "Background" (amber) on
  Welcome loop, "1 Overlay" (green, lock icon) on Summit logo. Welcome words has no chip (foreground by default).
- Cards: number top-left; a coloured chip top-right when the slide is set to the background or an overlay (the READ
  ME slides show an amber **BG** chip because their background is set in the editor); under the picture a **Text**
  badge (or Video) and the slide's name. Selected card = indigo border; the card that is out = orange border.
  Welcome loop's card is a video card named "Welcome loop" with a Video badge.
- Empty presentation (My Stuff › New Presentation) shows a grey "Slides (cue sheet) — coming soon" placeholder.

## Top — the band (shot 01, 02)
- **Monitor tile** at the left: header = tab **01** (orange digits while on air) + screen name
  **Virtual screen 1920×1080**; power switch (orange toggle) at the right end of the header.
- **Preview** (16:9) under the header. **Layers shown** strip under the preview: cells **B F 1 2 3 4 5** in their
  colours (B amber, F indigo, 1–5 green).
- **Layer column** to the right of the preview, top→bottom: **CLEAR**, then **1 2 3 4 5 F B**. A cell fills with
  its colour while something is on it (shot 02: 1 green, F indigo, B amber) and an **×** appears beside it.
  A white outline marks the layer the controls act on.
- **Control row** under the tile: time · progress bar · remaining time; play/pause · red ■ · speed **1.0×** ·
  a short coloured bar with the layer's name (**F Foreground** / **B Background**) · a sliders button
  (**Stage output**, only with one screen) · mute · volume.
- **Meter**: vertical bar with the master handle, **0.0 dB**, buttons **MUTE** and **SOLO**.
- **Music** panel: heading **Music**, folder and **+** icons, a folder bar **Default**, "Drop music here",
  its own transport (play, ■, 1.0×, mute, volume).
- **Slide settings** panel at the right end: heading **Slide settings** with "Option + slide to load" beside it,
  an empty preview, a **To Screen** button.

## Bottom — status bar (shot 07 crop, 01)
Left→right: **Show** | **Edit** (segmented) · monitor icon · chip **● On air 1/1 · Screen 1** (orange dot) ·
speaker icon + **LG HDR 4K** · dot + stage title (**Stage clear** grey when nothing is up; **Welcome** with an
orange dot while the Welcome slide is up) · **Tell us what's wrong** (envelope button) · card-size slider ·
pencil (Lock editing) · gear (Settings). No Update chip, no wrench.

## Edit (shot 04)
Press **Edit** → the window becomes the slide editor: **Show on output** button top-left, slide strip down the
left (New Slide / Duplicate slide at its bottom), ribbon rows above a large slide canvas, **Background** row at
the bottom. Status bar now has **Edit** filled and a presentation picker ("Start here"). Press **Show** to return.
