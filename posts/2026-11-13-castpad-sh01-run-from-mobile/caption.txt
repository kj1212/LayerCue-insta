Run your ATEM from mobile.

CastPad is a remote control app for Blackmagic ATEM switchers, on your phone or tablet. It talks to your switcher directly, over your own network: no cloud, no account. Choose your next shot in Preview, then take it with CUT or AUTO. And the layout is yours to build.

Try it in your browser, no install: castpad.app/demo
