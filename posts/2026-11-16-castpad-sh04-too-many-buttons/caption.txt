Too many buttons? Keep only what your show needs.

In CastPad, the remote control app for Blackmagic ATEM switchers on your phone or tablet, press and hold a source button, then drop it on the grey plus button to hide it. Need it back? Tap plus and pick it. It's a real take: it goes straight to air, and the button returns to its row. To move whole cards, open + Card › Layout edit, drag a card, and tap Layout done.

None of this changes the switcher itself. Your layout is saved per model, on that device.
