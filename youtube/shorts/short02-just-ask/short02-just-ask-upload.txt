TITLE
What if you could just ask? AI builds your slides in LayerCue #Shorts

DESCRIPTION
Connect your AI app to LayerCue over MCP (Claude, Cursor, LM Studio) and say "make a presentation that explains LayerCue's features." It builds the slides straight into LayerCue, checks what LayerCue actually drew, and lines them up in a cue sheet.

Libraries for your slides. Cue sheets for the night. Click once, and it's live on the big screen.

Free while it's in beta, on Mac and Windows: https://layercue.com

The narration and music were made with AI running on a Mac.
