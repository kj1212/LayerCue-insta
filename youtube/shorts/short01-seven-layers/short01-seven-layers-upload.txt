TITLE
Seven layers. Every screen. #Shorts

DESCRIPTION
A background that loops underneath, your slides on top, a logo and a name over it all. In LayerCue each one is its own layer, and each one goes live with a single click.

Send the show to every screen in the room, and give each screen only the layers it needs.

Free while it's in beta, on Mac and Windows: https://layercue.com
Full tutorial series: LayerCue from Zero

The narration and music were made with AI running on a Mac. The event in the demo is made up.
