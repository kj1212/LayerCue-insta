TITLE
AI Builds Your Slides, Plus Files and Settings | LayerCue from Zero, Episode 15

DESCRIPTION
Let an AI build your slides: in a new Claude chat, one sentence makes a five-slide talk with every picture drawn as vector art, right inside LayerCue. You'll also keep a show safe and movable. In Settings › Files & YouTube, bring a video that still plays from a USB stick into the media folder with Import external files, then open the LayerCue folder: libraries, cue sheets and LayerCue's own copies of your media are ordinary files. Back it up by quitting and copying the folder, move it by putting it in the same place on another computer, and know that Backups is one step back, not a history. Settings stay on the computer (Keep settings in my account brings most along), and Reset has no undo. On a narrow window Slide settings and Music fold, and a folded heading opens the panel in its own window. Finally, AI helpers: Settings › AI apps connects Claude Desktop, LM Studio or Cursor — and in a new Claude chat, one sentence asks for a five-slide school talk on the Solar System. Claude draws every picture as vector art and builds the talk in LayerCue on its own. The slides are Claude's real, unedited work; it took about five minutes, and the video cuts the wait and shows the finished chat. Without a connection, Copy as HTML for AI lets any AI chat rewrite your slides and paste them back.
New here? Start with Episode 1: Meet LayerCue — The Main Window.
Recorded on LayerCue 0.9.12.
Manual: https://layercue.com/docs/help/files/ · https://layercue.com/docs/settings/ · https://layercue.com/docs/ai/
The narration is AI-generated and the music is built from GarageBand loops. The event and people in the demo are fictional.

DESCRIPTION
AI에게 슬라이드를 맡겨 보세요. Claude 새 대화에 한 문장이면, 그림까지 모두 벡터로 그린 다섯 장 발표가 LayerCue 안에 생겨요. 쇼를 안전하게, 옮길 수 있게 지키는 법도 알려 드려요. 처음이라면 1편 「LayerCue 메인 창」부터 보세요.
LayerCue 0.9.12로 녹화했어요.
