TITLE
Text Slides in the Editor | LayerCue from Zero, Episode 9

DESCRIPTION
Make text slides in the LayerCue editor: opening and leaving it (no Save button), the left column and the four-row ribbon, adding and typing in a text box, finding a font and setting its size, making white text readable over a bright photo with Outline and Text shadow, what to do when text doesn't fit, and sending from the editor while your edits follow live.
New here? Start with Episode 1: Meet LayerCue — The Main Window.
Recorded on LayerCue 0.9.12.
Manual: https://layercue.com/docs/slides/text/
The narration is AI-generated and the music is built from GarageBand loops. The choir and photo in the demo are fictional.
