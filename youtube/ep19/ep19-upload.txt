TITLE
What's New in LayerCue 0.9.14: Update Output, Gradient Text and Shape Handles

DESCRIPTION
LayerCue 0.9.14 is out. Here's what's new, one feature at a time.
• Update output: edit the slide that's on air and the audience screen keeps showing it as it was. The button at the top left turns into Update output — press it and your changes go out in one go. Turn on the lightning bolt for live updates, as before.
• Gradient text: in Text color, pick Linear or Radial. The gradient spreads over the letters, not the box.
• Round and slant: the yellow dot rounds the corners of a text box or rectangle. Click the link next to it and each corner gets its own dot, so you can round only the corners you want. The light-blue handles slant it side to side or up and down, and the rounding stays.
• Auto-dismiss bar: a slide on an overlay with Auto-dismiss shows a bar on its card that fills as the time runs out.
• Changed: the layer strips now read 1 2 3 4 5 F B, the same order as the layer column. Each cell still switches the same layer.
Full list: https://layercue.com/changelog/

LayerCue 0.9.14 새 기능: Update output, 그라데이션 글자, 도형 핸들

LayerCue 0.9.14가 나왔어요. 새로 생긴 것들을 하나씩 볼게요.
• Update output: 송출 중인 슬라이드를 고쳐도 관객 화면은 그대로예요. 왼쪽 위 버튼이 Update output으로 바뀌고, 누르면 고친 내용이 한 번에 나가요. 번개 버튼을 켜면 예전처럼 바로 반영돼요.
