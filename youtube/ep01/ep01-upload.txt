TITLE
Meet LayerCue: The Main Window | LayerCue from Zero, Episode 1

DESCRIPTION
A friendly four-minute tour of the LayerCue main window: what every part is called, what it does, and how layers work.
Not installed yet? Episode 2 walks you through it, on Mac and Windows.
Recorded on LayerCue 0.9.10.
No account is needed to run a show; Settings needs a one-time Google sign-in.
Manual: https://layercue.com/docs/start/tour/
The narration is AI-generated and the music is built from GarageBand loops. The event in the demo, Riverside Tech Summit 2026, is fictional; the demo is scripted on a virtual screen.
