# Episode 1 — Meet LayerCue: The Main Window (v2, 4:08)

## S1 · What LayerCue is

1. Hi there! This is LayerCue.
2. It puts your show on the big screen, in layers.
3. It runs on Mac and Windows.
4. Here, a video runs underneath.
5. Words sit on top.
6. And a logo stays up in the corner.
7. Each one comes and goes on its own.
8. In this episode, we'll walk around the main window.
9. Today we just look.
10. You won't send anything out yet.

## S2 · The four areas

1. The window has four areas.
2. The sidebar on the left.
3. Your slides in the middle.
4. The stage band across the top.
5. And the status bar along the bottom.
6. Here's an easy way to remember it.
7. Pick on the left.
8. Click in the middle.
9. Watch the top.
10. Adjust on the right.
11. Adjust means Slide settings, at the far right of the band.

## S3 · The sidebar

1. Let's start on the left.
2. Cue Sheets are at the top.
3. A cue sheet is the running order for one event.
4. This one, Tour, lines up six short guides.
5. Below the line is the Library drawer.
6. A library is a folder of presentations.
7. And a presentation is one set of slides.
8. The number on the right is how many slides it has.
9. Press the double arrow to pull the drawer up.
10. Press it again to lower it.
11. Just pointing at it won't raise it.

## S4 · The middle: slides

1. Now I'll click Start here, inside the Tour cue sheet.
2. The middle shows every presentation in it, in order.
3. Each one has a title bar with its name and slide count.
4. On the right are five buttons.
5. Add File.
6. Link.
7. Edit.
8. Layer for this list.
9. And Presentation settings.
10. Below the bar are the slide cards.
11. Under each picture, you'll see its kind and its name.
12. Here's the big one: a click on the picture sends it out.
13. No confirmation, no second step.
14. So we won't click any pictures today.
15. That's Episode 3.

## S5 · The top band

1. Up top is where you watch the stage.
2. The tile on the left is Screen 1.
3. Here it's a virtual screen, so no projector is needed.
4. The switch at the right end turns the output on and off.
5. Under the preview, Layers shown picks what this monitor shows.
6. Beside the preview, the layer column shows what's up right now.
7. Clear at the top, then seven layers.
8. Five overlays.
9. F for foreground.
10. And B for background.
11. Next comes the sound meter.
12. Then Music, which has its own channel.
13. And at the right end, Slide settings.
14. Option-click a slide to look at it here, without sending it.

## S6 · The status bar

1. The status bar reads from left to right.
2. Show and Edit.
3. The On air chip counts the monitors that are showing.
4. The speaker box is your sound device.
5. The dot and title tell you what's on stage.
6. Tell us what's wrong sends us a note.
7. The slider changes the card size.
8. The pencil is Lock editing.
9. And the gear opens Settings.

## S7 · Layers, in a demo

1. Now let's see layers in action.
2. This demo is scripted, and the event is made up.
3. A video goes to the background.
4. The B cell turns amber.
5. Words go on top, in the foreground.
6. F turns indigo.
7. A logo goes to overlay one.
8. And cell 1 turns green.
9. Now I'll change the words.
10. The video keeps playing.
11. The little x next to F takes just the words down.
12. That's the whole idea of layers!

## S8 · Edit and back

1. One last stop: Edit, in the status bar.
2. The whole window becomes the slide editor.
3. Your slides line up in a strip down the left.
4. Click Show to come back.
5. And that's the main window!

## S9 · What's next

1. You can now find your way around the whole window.
2. Next up: Episode 2, Install, First Launch and Settings.
3. See you there!
