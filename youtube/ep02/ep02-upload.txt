TITLE
Install, First Launch and Settings | LayerCue from Zero, Episode 2

DESCRIPTION
Get LayerCue onto your Mac or Windows PC, see what's there the first time you open it, sign in once for Settings, and learn the General, Files and Tools pages (including where your media goes) and how to quit safely.
New here? Start with Episode 1: Meet LayerCue — The Main Window.
Recorded on LayerCue 0.9.12.
Mac download: https://layercue.com/download/  ·  Windows: Microsoft Store (search LayerCue, published by CastPad)
Manual: https://layercue.com/docs/start/install/
The narration is AI-generated and the music is built from GarageBand loops. The install screens are illustrations; the sign-in panel is from the manual.
