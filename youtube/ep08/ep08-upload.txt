TITLE
Presentation Settings: Fades, Auto-dismiss and Layer for This List | LayerCue from Zero, Episode 8

DESCRIPTION
Set the four values a whole presentation carries: Crossfade and Fade out (how slides come and go), Auto-dismiss (overlays that take themselves down), and Layer for this list (every slide on one layer). Plus the difference between following the defaults in Settings and a presentation's own value.
New here? Start with Episode 1: Meet LayerCue — The Main Window.
Recorded on LayerCue 0.9.12.
Manual: https://layercue.com/docs/library/presentation-settings/
The narration is AI-generated and the music is built from GarageBand loops. The event, people and media in the demo are fictional.
