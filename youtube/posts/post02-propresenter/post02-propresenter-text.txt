TEXT (ENGLISH)
Priced out of ProPresenter? A license for every computer adds up — for a school hall, a small venue, a conference room, a church, or anyone running the screen from the back of the room. Especially when most nights you use a fraction of it.

So here's the honest version.

If you use all of ProPresenter, keep it. It's good software, and it does far more than we do.

But if most nights you're cueing a background that loops, the words, a caption and a logo, a YouTube video, and a second screen that behaves — that's the part LayerCue does, and it's free while it's in beta. Mac and Windows. Bring your PowerPoint: it opens as slides you can still edit.

Try it at your next show, before you renew. And tell us what broke. https://layercue.com
